Bouquets

Every bouquet is made to order, from fresh seasonal flowers. We take orders by phone and by email.

  • Signature bouquets

    Designed around the occasion and the person. You choose the mood, the colour and the budget.

  • Classic bouquets

    Roses, tulips, peonies, carnations, gerberas — a single species, a clean form.

  • Wedding bouquets

    The bridal bouquet with buttonholes and bouquets for the bridesmaids.

  • Dried and preserved flowers

    No water, no watering. Longevity measured in years.

  • Recurring and corporate bouquets

    A standing delivery of fresh bouquets to an office, restaurant, hotel or home.
